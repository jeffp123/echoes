Version 1.1

May 2026

  • Smarter frequency picker. Selecting how often an Echo appears is now clearer: five labeled steps replace the slider, and a short description tells you exactly what each setting means.
  • Show or hide completed Echoes. A toggle at the bottom of today's list lets you hide items you've already checked off. Your preference is remembered. After finishing everything, tap "Show completed" on the done screen to review what you accomplished.
  • Tapping a checkbox takes effect immediately. Tap again to uncheck.
  • List order stays stable. Echoes no longer move when checked off. The list stays in the same order throughout the day.
  • Day reset time now works correctly. Custom reset times (set under Settings) were not being respected in some cases. Fixed.
  • Appearance changes take effect immediately. Switching between Light, Dark, and System mode in Settings now updates the screen without needing to close and reopen Settings.
  • Sample Echoes appear right away. Loading sample Echoes from Settings now adds them to today's list immediately, without needing to reset the day.
  • Duplicate sample Echoes are skipped. If you load samples more than once, only new ones are added.
  • Various small fixes to text layout, keyboard dismissal, and background behavior.
