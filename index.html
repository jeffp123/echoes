How it works

Three simple ideas, nothing more.

1

Build your library

Write reminders in your own words — habits you want to keep, quotes that matter to you, things you need to hear. Organize them into categories and set how often each one should appear.

2

See today's list

Each morning, Echoes selects a subset of your library based on each reminder's frequency setting. The list is stable all day — it won't change if you close and reopen the app.

3

Check things off

Tap a reminder when you've thought about it, done it, or just acknowledged it. That's the whole interaction. Tomorrow brings a fresh list.

The philosophy

Echoes is deliberately minimal.

No streaks

Missing a day doesn't break anything. There's no score, no history, no guilt. Only today's list matters.

No history

Yesterday's list is gone. The app doesn't track what you've done or how often — it just keeps surfacing what you told it to surface.

Your words

Every reminder is something you wrote yourself. No suggested content, no templates, no default library to edit around.

Frequency, not scheduling

Instead of scheduling reminders for specific times, you set how often each one should appear — Occasionally, Weekly-ish, Every few days, Often, or Daily. The algorithm handles the rest.

Features

  • Probability-based schedulingEach reminder's frequency drives how often it appears, without fixed dates or times.
  • CategoriesOrganize your library into any categories you choose.
  • Expandable detailsAdd backstory or context to any reminder, visible inline when you need it.
  • 4-second undoTap to check off; a brief window to undo before it commits.
  • iCloud syncYour library and today's list stay in sync across all your devices.
  • Daily notificationOptional morning nudge at a time you set.
  • Import / exportFull library as JSON — back up, restore, or hand-edit your reminders.
  • AppearanceSystem, light, or dark mode.
