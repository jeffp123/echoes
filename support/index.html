Frequently asked questions

How do I add a reminder?

Tap the Reminders tab, then the + button in the top-right corner. Give your reminder some text, assign it a category, and set how often you want it to appear. Tap Save.

What does the frequency setting do?

Each reminder has a frequency from Occasionally to Daily. This controls how often the reminder appears in your daily list:

  • Occasionally — roughly once a week or less
  • Weekly-ish — a few times a week
  • Every few days — every couple of days on average
  • Often — most days
  • Daily — every day

The algorithm is probability-based, so these are tendencies rather than guarantees.

Why didn't a reminder appear today?

If a reminder's frequency is set below Daily, it won't appear every day — that's intentional. If a reminder set to Daily is missing, check that it's marked as Active (tap it in the Reminders tab to open the edit form).

How do I reset today's list?

Open Settings (the gear icon on either tab) and scroll to the Danger Zone section. Tap Reset Today's List. This rebuilds today's selection immediately.

Can I edit or delete a reminder?

Yes. Tap any reminder in the Reminders tab to open the edit form. From there you can change any field, toggle it active/inactive, or delete it with the Delete Reminder button at the bottom.

How does iCloud sync work?

If you're signed into the same Apple ID on multiple devices, your entire reminder library and today's checked state sync automatically via iCloud. There's nothing to set up — sync is on by default.

How do I back up my reminders?

Open Settings and tap Export Reminders to share a JSON file containing your full library. You can re-import this file later with Import Reminders (note: importing replaces your current library).

Can I use Echoes without iCloud?

Yes. If you're not signed into iCloud or have iCloud disabled for Echoes, the app works entirely locally on your device.

How do I cancel or get a refund for a purchase?

All purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support.
